History / Geschiedenis: 10 epochs (tijdvakken)

Self-study guides in English and Dutch (brugklas VWO+, first year of secondary school). The aim is to see history as one connected picture: each epoch starts with what it inherited from the one before and ends with what it hands on to the next. Every epoch has the same tools: kenmerkende aspecten and windows of the canon, a chain of causes, “why this way?” and “what if?” questions, a crossroads choice, a lab model, flashcards and a practice test. Each page has a switch at the top to show English, English + Dutch, or Dutch only, and an epoch switcher.

The question that runs through all 10 epochs: who produces the surplus, and who controls it?

  1. Tijd van jagers en boeren (until 3000 BC)
    Hunter-gatherers, the agricultural revolution, surplus and the first cities, the Netherlands in prehistory (hunebeds).
  2. Tijd van Grieken en Romeinen (3000 BC – AD 500)
    The Greek polis and democracy, science and philosophy, the Roman Empire, Romans and Germanic peoples in the Netherlands, Christianity, the fall of Rome.
  3. Tijd van monniken en ridders (500 – 1000)
    The manor and serfs, the Franks and feudalism, monasteries and missions, Islam, Dorestad and the Vikings.
  4. Tijd van steden en staten (1000 – 1500)
    Trade and towns, burghers and the Hanseatic League, pope and emperor, the Crusades, centralisation, water and peat in the Netherlands.
  5. Tijd van ontdekkers en hervormers (1500 – 1600)
    The voyages of discovery, the Renaissance and humanism (Erasmus), the Reformation, the Dutch Revolt and the birth of the Republic.
  6. Tijd van regenten en vorsten (1600 – 1700)
    Absolute monarchs, the regents’ Republic, the VOC and world trade, the Golden Age, the Scientific Revolution.
  7. Tijd van pruiken en revoluties (1700 – 1800)
    The Enlightenment, the old order, plantations and slavery, revolutions in America, France and the Netherlands (the Patriots, the Batavian Republic).
  8. Tijd van burgers en stoommachines (1800 – 1900)
    The Industrial Revolution, the social question, liberalism, socialism, confessionalism, the 1848 constitution, imperialism and the Dutch East Indies.
  9. Tijd van wereldoorlogen (1900 – 1950)
    The First World War, the 1929 crisis, totalitarianism, the Second World War and the Holocaust, the occupation of the Netherlands, Indonesia.
  10. Tijd van televisie en computer (1950 – now)
    The Cold War, decolonisation, the welfare state, the sixties, European unification, a multicultural society, the internet.
